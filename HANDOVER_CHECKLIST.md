# Handover checklist

- Confirm repository owner/URL and protected branches.
- Confirm Cloudflare account, dedicated D1/R2 names and access policy.
- Confirm identity provider, role mapping and department owners.
- Confirm NAS access, scheduler host, retention and recovery targets.
- Import only approved real data and remove TEST DATA before production.
- Pass clean clone, install, typecheck, lint, tests, build, permission, accessibility and restore drill.
- Record credentials by location/owner only; never include their values.
